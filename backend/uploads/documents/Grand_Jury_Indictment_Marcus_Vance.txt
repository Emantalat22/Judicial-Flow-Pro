SUPERIOR COURT OF THE STATE — CRIMINAL INDICTMENT
THE PEOPLE OF THE STATE v. MARCUS VANCE, Defendant.
COUNT ONE: WIRE FRAUD (18 U.S.C. 1343)
From January 2024 to November 2025, Defendant orchestrated a scheme to divert $8,400,000 from the Municipal Transit Pension Fund into offshore shell corporations under the guise of private equity capital commitments.
