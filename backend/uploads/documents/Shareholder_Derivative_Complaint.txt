SHAREHOLDER DERIVATIVE ACTION FOR BREACH OF FIDUCIARY DUTIES
1. Plaintiffs bring this action on behalf of Quantum BioPharma Inc against Sterling Ventures Corp.
2. Defendants failed to disclose an FDA Clinical Hold letter dated November 14, 2025 regarding drug candidate QBP-401.
3. Controlling shareholders executed stock redemption agreements at $45.00/share days before public disclosure collapsed the stock to $11.20/share.
