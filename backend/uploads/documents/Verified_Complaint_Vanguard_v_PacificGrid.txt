IN THE DISTRICT COURT OF RECORD — COMMERCIAL DIVISION
VANGUARD RENEWABLE ENERGY LLC, Plaintiff, v. PACIFIC GRID CORP, Defendant.
Case No. JFP-2026-0101

VERIFIED COMPLAINT FOR INJUNCTIVE RELIEF AND DAMAGES

1. Plaintiff Vanguard Renewable Energy operates a 500-megawatt solar and battery storage facility.
2. Under the Master Interconnection Agreement dated June 12, 2024, Defendant Pacific Grid was contractually obligated to complete substation telemetry synchronization by October 1, 2025 (Section 4.2).
3. Defendant intentionally delayed interconnection by 14 weeks while secretly extracting proprietary battery load balancing algorithms and firmware source code (Version 4.2.1) under the pretext of safety testing.
4. Plaintiff has suffered liquidated damages exceeding $24,500,000 and imminent loss of intellectual property exclusivity.
5. WHEREFORE, Plaintiff moves for preliminary injunction and immediate inspection of substation telemetry records.
