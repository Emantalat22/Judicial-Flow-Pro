DEFENDANT PACIFIC GRID CORP'S ANSWER AND AFFIRMATIVE DEFENSES
1. Defendant denies all allegations of bad faith delay and trade secret misappropriation.
2. FIRST AFFIRMATIVE DEFENSE: Interconnection holds were instituted pursuant to mandatory State Reliability Directive SR-902.
3. SECOND AFFIRMATIVE DEFENSE: Vanguard's inverters generated excessive third-order harmonic distortion exceeding 5.2% THD.
COUNTERCLAIM: Defendant claims $4,200,000 for emergency reactive power compensation installed at Substation Alpha-7.
